[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Frost Owl" (Gufo del gelo). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests (frost lands).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 25 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE owl per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. The body is always centred in its cell, at the same height; only wings, limbs and tail move.
The creature: a round stocky owl of the frozen lands, seen from the side: a big round head with two short feathery EAR TUFTS, a wide face disc with one huge glowing round eye, a small golden hooked beak, a plump body with soft scale-like feathers, big broad rounded wings and short talons. Frost-blue plumage, darker on the wings, lighter on the chest, with a few white frost speckles on the belly. A night hunter that spits shards of ice. Colours: dark frost blue wings (#294766, #0a2933 for the darkest feathers), frost blue body (#477099), light blue-grey chest (#7aadcc), pale ice-white speckles and ear tufts (#ccf5ff), golden beak and talons (#ebc247). Eyes: one HUGE round glowing mint-green eye (#8ff5d6) with a dark pupil, the brightest thing on the owl. Its glowing parts (the big mint-green eye (#8ff5d6)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 flying cycle 1/4: wings fully UP above the body; 2 flying cycle 2/4: wings half down; 3 flying cycle 3/4: wings fully DOWN below the body; 4 flying cycle 4/4: wings half up again.
Row 2: 5 hovering in place, wings (or fins) spread, calm, looking right; 6 gliding downward, wings spread wide and still, body tilted slightly nose-down; 7 attacking: head raised, mouth (or its glowing organ) wide open, firing a fan of sharp pale-blue ICE SHARDS from its open beak; 8 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut.
