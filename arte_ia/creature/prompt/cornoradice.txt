[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Rootgoat" (Cornoradice). It is gentle and harmless unless provoked: soft round shapes, calm eyes. It lives in the surface forests (lantern forest, amber woods).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 35 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 3 rows and 4 columns: 12 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE goat per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a sturdy wild goat-like beast of the lantern forest: a stocky body with a hump of thick shaggy brown fur on the shoulders, strong legs with dark hooves, a short tail, a long head with a short beard; on its head two big curved HORNS made of twisted living ROOT wood (lighter tan bark with a few tiny turquoise sap buds), curving back and up. Usually calm and grazing, but when provoked it lowers its root horns and charges. Colours: brown fur (#8f5233, darker #663d29 on the hump, #47291f underneath), light tan muzzle and belly (#cc8f66), root-wood horns (#a37047, tips #cc8f66) with tiny turquoise sap buds (#3da38f), dark hooves (#2a1810). Eyes: one round glowing mint-turquoise eye (#8ff5d6). Its glowing parts (light teal (#8ff5d6)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, back leg back; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other front leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 preparing to charge: head down, front foot scraping the ground, back legs bent; 7 charging: body stretched long and low, legs spread in a gallop, head forward; 8 eating: head lowered to the ground, nibbling a small green leaf.
Row 3: 9 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes); 10 alert: standing taller, looking right, ears/antennae up; 11 idle variant: blinking, head slightly turned; 12 idle variant: breathing, slightly different from the other idle pose.
