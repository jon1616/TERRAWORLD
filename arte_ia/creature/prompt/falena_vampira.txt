[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Vampire Moth" (Falena vampira). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests, only at night.
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 22 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE moth per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. The body is always centred in its cell, at the same height; only wings, limbs and tail move.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a sinister night moth that latches onto its prey and drinks its life, seen from the side: a furry dark body, a small head with a long sharp curled PROBOSCIS like a needle, two feathery antennae, thin legs with hooked tips, and big ragged wings with torn edges and two pale bone-coloured EYE-SPOTS on each wing. Dark crimson and black, creepy. Colours: very dark crimson-black body and wing bases (#200a0e, #3a1218), deep blood-red wings (#6a1a24, lighter #9a2a34 at the edges), pale bone-white eye-spots on the wings (#e8d8c8). Eyes: one small glowing bright red eye (#ff3040). Its glowing parts (red (#ff3352)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 flying cycle 1/4: wings fully UP above the body; 2 flying cycle 2/4: wings half down; 3 flying cycle 3/4: wings fully DOWN below the body; 4 flying cycle 4/4: wings half up again.
Row 2: 5 hovering in place, wings (or fins) spread, calm, looking right; 6 gliding downward, wings spread wide and still, body tilted slightly nose-down; 7 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes); 8 hovering variant: wings (or fins) slightly LOWER than in the other hovering pose.
