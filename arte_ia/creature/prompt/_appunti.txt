[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Peat Frog" (Rana di torba). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests (torba).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 22 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE frog per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a plump squat frog of the peat bogs, seen from the side: a wide flat head with a big mouth and one bulging round eye on top, a round body, strong folded back legs and short front legs, smooth damp dark-green skin with a few round GLOWING turquoise SPOTS on its back, a pale green belly. It jumps toward you in big leaps. Colours: dark bog-green skin (#2a5a40, darker #1a3a2a, darkest #0e2018), lighter green on the head (#4a8a60), pale green belly (#90d0a0), glowing turquoise spots (#5cf0d8). Eyes: one big bulging glowing turquoise eye (#5cf0d8) with a dark pupil. Its glowing parts (the turquoise spots and the eye (#5cf0d8)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 standing still, relaxed, breathing; 2 idle variant: a little wider and lower (breathing out), eyes half closed; 3 crouching before the jump: squashed low and wide, eyes on the target; 4 take-off: stretched tall and narrow, leaving the ground, leaning forward.
Row 2: 5 in the air at the top of the jump: compact and round, legs tucked, lifted HIGH in the cell; 6 falling: stretched slightly downward, legs reaching for the ground, lifted a little; 7 landing: squashed very flat and wide on the ground, wobbling; 8 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes).
