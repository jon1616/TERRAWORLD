[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Amber Fox" (Volpe d'ambra). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests (amber woods, lantern forest, rossa).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 30 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE fox per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a lean clever fox of the amber woods, a predator: a slim body on long thin legs, a narrow pointed muzzle, big pointed ears, and a huge fluffy tail whose TIP is a translucent glowing drop of AMBER resin, like a little lantern. Warm amber-orange fur, darker along the back, a cream chest and muzzle tip, dark 'stockings' on the legs. Sly, fast, it pounces on its prey. Colours: amber-orange fur (#eb993d, darker #ad5c1f on the back), cream chest and muzzle (#ffcc8f), dark brown legs and ear tips (#663314), glowing amber tail tip (#ffb040 with a pale #ffe6a0 centre), black nose (#141414). Eyes: one narrow sly golden eye (#ffd060) with a dark slit pupil.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, back leg back; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other front leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 preparing to charge: head down, front foot scraping the ground, back legs bent; 7 charging: body stretched long and low, legs spread in a gallop, head forward; 8 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes).
