[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Spore Blob" (Grumo di spore). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests, the slate caverns, the sap depths (swamp).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 20 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE blob per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
The creature: a small hopping blob of living swamp jelly, cousin of the Moss Blob: a soft round dome of translucent jelly, one big white shine spot on the upper left, two big dark oval eyes on the right side; on top of its head grow three small round MUSHROOM CAPS with pale spots, and inside the jelly float a few big glowing SPORE DOTS. It is heavier and grumpier than the Moss Blob and jumps higher. Colours: deep indigo-blue jelly (#2a2f7a dark bottom, #4a56c8 body, #9aa8ff light top), pale lavender-white mushroom caps with indigo spots (#e8ecff, #4a56c8), glowing spore dots of pale turquoise (#8ff0e0). Eyes: two big dark oval eyes (#14141f) with a tiny white shine.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
NO purple, NO pink, NO magenta anywhere (they vanish on the background): use indigo-blue instead.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 standing still, relaxed, breathing; 2 idle variant: a little wider and lower (breathing out), eyes half closed; 3 crouching before the jump: squashed low and wide, eyes on the target; 4 take-off: stretched tall and narrow, leaving the ground, leaning forward.
Row 2: 5 in the air at the top of the jump: compact and round, legs tucked, lifted HIGH in the cell; 6 falling: stretched slightly downward, legs reaching for the ground, lifted a little; 7 landing: squashed very flat and wide on the ground, wobbling; 8 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut.
