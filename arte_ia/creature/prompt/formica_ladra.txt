[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Amber Thief Ant" (Formica ladra d'ambra). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests (amber woods).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 20 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE ant per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
The creature: a big ant of the amber woods, about the size of a cat, a sneaky little thief: three clearly separated body parts (a round head with two long bent antennae and strong mandibles, a slim middle with six thin jointed legs, and a big drop-shaped abdomen), the abdomen made of translucent glossy AMBER with two pale golden stripes across it, like a drop of resin. Shiny armour, alert antennae, cheeky look. Colours: dark brown head and legs (#3a2208, #6a3e10), glossy amber body (#a0621a, #d8962a), pale golden stripes and shine (#fff0a8). Eyes: one small round black eye (#101010) with a white glint.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: legs in a wide stance; 2 walking cycle 2/4: middle legs forward; 3 walking cycle 3/4: front and back legs forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs together.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 running away fast with a small stolen shiny trinket in its mouth: body stretched low, legs spread in a leap; 7 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 8 alert: standing taller, looking right, ears/antennae up.
