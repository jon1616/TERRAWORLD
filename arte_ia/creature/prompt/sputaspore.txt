[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Spore Spitter" (Sputaspore). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests, the sap depths, the bottom of the world (swamp).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 20 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE mushroom per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a living MUSHROOM rooted in the swamp ground: a thick short dark-brown stalk with little roots spreading at the base, and a big round bulbous cap like a puffball, with one big pale round PORE on its front side that opens to shoot spores. The cap is covered in a few big pale spots. It does not walk: it stays rooted and pulses, then spits a lob of glowing spores. Colours: deep indigo-blue cap (#2a2f7a dark, #4a56c8 body, #9aa8ff light top), pale lavender-white spots and pore (#e8ecff), dark brown stalk and roots (#47291f, #29140f). Eyes: no eyes: its big pale pore on the front of the cap is its 'face'. Its glowing parts (the pore and the spots (#e8ecff, #9aa8ff)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
NO purple, NO pink, NO magenta anywhere (they vanish on the background): use indigo-blue instead.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 resting, relaxed, breathing; 2 breathing in: slightly swollen and taller; 3 breathing out: slightly smaller and lower; 4 about to shoot: swollen, glowing parts brighter, bracing.
Row 2: 5 shooting: bursting open, firing a lob of glowing pale-blue spores out of its pore upward and forward; 6 idle variant: swaying slightly to one side; 7 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes); 8 idle variant: tilted slightly to the other side.
