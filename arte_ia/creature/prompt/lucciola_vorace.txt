[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Ravenous Firefly" (Lucciola vorace). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests, only at night.
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 18 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE firefly per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. The body is always centred in its cell, at the same height; only wings, limbs and tail move.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a small aggressive night firefly that hunts in swarms, seen from the side: a dark armoured head with big hooked MANDIBLES and two thin antennae, a short dark body, two small pale translucent wings, and a big round glowing LIME-GREEN abdomen. Mean, hungry look. Colours: dark olive-black head and body (#29291f), glowing lime-green abdomen (#d6ff70 with a brighter #f0ffb0 core), pale bluish wings (#ccf5ff). Eyes: one small glowing RED eye (#ff523d). Its glowing parts (the lime-green abdomen (#d6ff70) and the red eye (#ff523d)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 flying cycle 1/4: wings fully UP above the body; 2 flying cycle 2/4: wings half down; 3 flying cycle 3/4: wings fully DOWN below the body; 4 flying cycle 4/4: wings half up again.
Row 2: 5 hovering in place, wings (or fins) spread, calm, looking right; 6 gliding downward, wings spread wide and still, body tilted slightly nose-down; 7 hurt: flinching back, squeezed, eyes shut (if it has eyes); 8 hovering variant: wings (or fins) slightly LOWER than in the other hovering pose.
