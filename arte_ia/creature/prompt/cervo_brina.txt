[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Frost Stag" (Cervo di brina). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests (frost lands).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 30 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 3 rows and 4 columns: 12 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE deer per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
The creature: a slender elegant deer of the frozen lands: a lean body with long thin legs, a proud neck and a narrow head; its coat is frost-blue, darker on the back and lighter on the belly, sprinkled with a few big pale frost spots along the back; a short white tail; on its head a pair of ANTLERS made of clear pale ICE CRYSTALS, branching into 3-4 sharp points, big and clearly readable. Graceful but ready to fight: it lowers its icy antlers to charge. Colours: dark frost blue (#294766), frost blue (#477099), light blue-grey belly (#7aadcc), pale ice-white antlers and spots (#ccf5ff), dark hooves (#1a2a3a). Eyes: one round glowing mint-green eye (#8ff5d6). Its glowing parts (light teal (#8ff5d6), light cyan blue (#ccf5ff)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, back leg back; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other front leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 preparing to charge: head down, front foot scraping the ground, back legs bent; 7 charging: body stretched long and low, legs spread in a gallop, head forward; 8 eating: head lowered to the ground, nibbling a small green leaf.
Row 3: 9 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 10 alert: standing taller, looking right, ears/antennae up; 11 idle variant: blinking, head slightly turned; 12 idle variant: breathing, slightly different from the other idle pose.
