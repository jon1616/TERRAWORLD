[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Glow Bee" (Ape di lume). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests (lantern forest, swamp, amber woods, prati).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 20 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE bee per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. The body is always centred in its cell, at the same height; only wings, limbs and tail move.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a plump round fuzzy bee of the lantern forest, seen from the side: a big round striped body (golden yellow with two or three thick dark brown bands), a small round dark head with two short curled antennae, one round dark eye, a short stinger whose TIP GLOWS like a tiny lantern, and two small round translucent pale wings on top. Cute but quick, it buzzes around flowers. Colours: golden yellow body (#ebc247, darker #ad851f), dark brown bands and head (#291f14), pale bluish-white wings (#d6f5ff), glowing pale yellow stinger tip (#fff5a3). Eyes: one round dark eye (#141414) with a tiny white glint. Its glowing parts (the glowing stinger tip (#fff5a3)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 flying cycle 1/4: wings fully UP above the body; 2 flying cycle 2/4: wings half down; 3 flying cycle 3/4: wings fully DOWN below the body; 4 flying cycle 4/4: wings half up again.
Row 2: 5 hovering in place, wings (or fins) spread, calm, looking right; 6 gliding downward, wings spread wide and still, body tilted slightly nose-down; 7 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 8 hovering variant: wings (or fins) slightly LOWER than in the other hovering pose.
