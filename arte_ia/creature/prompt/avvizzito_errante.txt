[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Withered Wanderer" (Avvizzito errante). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests, only at night.
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 18 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no motion blur, no speed lines, no sparkles around the creature, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE husk per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a tall thin shambling HUSK that wanders at night: a humanoid shape made of dry dead ROOTS and grey withered bark, twisted together like a dried-up scarecrow, hunched forward, with long thin arms hanging down to the knees ending in twig-like claws, thin legs, and a lumpy head like a dead knot of wood with TWO small glowing AMBER eyes in a dark hollow. Grey-olive, cracked and dusty, sad and creepy (not gory). It walks slowly, dragging its feet. Colours: withered grey-olive bark (#70705c, lighter #8f857a, darker #525247), dead mossy patches (#66663d), very dark hollow face (#1a1a14). Eyes: two small glowing amber eyes (#f5ad3d) deep in a dark hollow. Its glowing parts (the two amber eyes (#f5ad3d)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, arms swinging; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 hurt: recoiling backwards, bark chips flying off, eyes flickering; 7 alert: standing taller, head raised, looking right, tense; 8 idle variant: blinking, head slightly turned.
