[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Frost Stoat" (Ermellino di brina). It is a wild creature of this world. It lives in the surface forests (frost lands).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 25 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE stoat per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
The creature: a small, long and slim stoat of the frozen lands, a cheeky little THIEF that steals shiny things and runs away: a long low body, short legs, a small triangular head with round ears and a pointed snout, a long bushy tail with a BLACK TIP. Its winter fur is snow-white with soft pale blue-grey shading on the back and legs. Quick, curious, mischievous expression. Colours: snow-white fur (#eef8ff), pale blue-grey shading (#c8dce8, #9ab0c0), darker blue-grey paws (#6a7a88), black tail tip and nose (#14141f). Eyes: one round shiny black eye (#101820) with a tiny white glint.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, back leg back; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other front leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 running away fast with a small stolen shiny trinket in its mouth: body stretched low, legs spread in a leap; 7 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut; 8 alert: standing taller, looking right, ears/antennae up.
