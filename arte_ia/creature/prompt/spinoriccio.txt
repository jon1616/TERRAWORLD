[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Thornhog" (Spinoriccio). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests, the root undergrowth caves (amber woods, lantern forest).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 20 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE hedgehog per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
The creature: a small round hedgehog-like creature of the amber woods: a plump dark-brown body with a pale pointed snout and a small pink nose, short stubby legs, and on its back a big crown of long GOLDEN AMBER THORNS (thick, few and clearly separated, like dried resin spikes, lighter at the tips). The thorns cover the back from the head to the tail and stick out in a wide fan, so the silhouette is spiky and unmistakable. Colours: dark brown body (#3d2914, #5c3d29, lighter #7a5233 on the face), golden amber thorns (#cc993d with pale yellow tips #f5d67a), a pale cream snout (#e8c8a0), a small pink nose (#eba3a3). Eyes: one small round bright amber-orange eye (#ffb040) with a dark pupil.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: front leg forward, back leg back; 2 walking cycle 2/4: legs passing under the body; 3 walking cycle 3/4: the other front leg forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs passing again.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 about to roll: hunched, tucking its head in, spikes/armour raised; 7 rolled up into a tight round ball (it rolls like a wheel), only its armour/spikes visible, PERFECTLY ROUND, the ball resting on the same ground line; 8 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut.
