[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap»] Side-view pixel-art game creature for a 2D side-scrolling game, organic hand-crafted look, a dark deep world where light comes from living things (amber, glowing sap, embers), NOT Terraria, NOT cute chibi, NOT 3D, NOT realistic painting.
This is the "Slate Beetle" (Scarabeo d'ardesia). It is aggressive: sharp shapes, a fierce eye. It lives in the surface forests, the slate caverns, the sap depths, the bottom of the world (amber woods).
Draw it LARGE with big clear shapes and flat colours: it will be pixelated later by a script to about 30 pixels wide, so every detail must be BIG and readable at that size (no thin lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no soft gradients, at most 2-3 tones per colour). No shadows on the ground, NO ground line, no dust clouds, no text, no numbers, no letters, no grid lines, no frames, no borders.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 2 rows and 4 columns: 8 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE beetle per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
EVERY cell shows the creature strictly in PROFILE (side view, as in a side-scrolling game), head on the RIGHT: never seen from above, never from below, never from the front, never facing the viewer.
The creature: a heavy armoured beetle made of SLATE STONE, seen from the side: a big domed shell of layered blue-grey slate plates with sharp edges, crossed by thin glowing EMBER-ORANGE cracks (like hot seams between the stone plates), a low head with a short thick HORN pointing forward for charging, two short antennae, six short sturdy legs. Heavy, slow, but it charges like a battering ram. Colours: blue-grey slate shell (#475c85, light #8599b8 on top edges, dark #293352 underneath), orange ember cracks (#d67a3d, bright #ffad70), dark legs and head (#3d4766). Eyes: one small glowing orange eye (#ffad70). Its glowing parts (the orange cracks and the eye (#ffad70)) shine like little lights in the dark: make them BIG, bright and clearly separate.
A thick very dark outline (almost black) all around the creature, one clean line.
Keep the SAME design in every cell: same proportions, same colours, same eye, same markings; only the pose changes. Every pose must read clearly as a silhouette.
Row 1: 1 walking cycle 1/4: legs in a wide stance; 2 walking cycle 2/4: middle legs forward; 3 walking cycle 3/4: front and back legs forward; 4 walking cycle 4/4: legs together.
Row 2: 5 standing still, relaxed, head up, breathing; 6 preparing to charge: head down, front foot scraping the ground, back legs bent; 7 charging: body stretched long and low, legs spread in a gallop, head forward; 8 hurt: flinching back, eyes squeezed shut.
