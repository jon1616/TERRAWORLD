Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 3 rows and 4 columns: 12 equal cells, NO extra row, NO extra copies, ONE animal per cell, ALWAYS facing RIGHT (head on the right), ALWAYS the same size and the same colours in every cell. In every cell the feet (or belly) touch the same ground line near the bottom of the cell, except the poses that are explicitly in the air.
