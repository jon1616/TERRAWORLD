Organic, slightly whimsical shapes: things look grown, carved or woven from roots, bark, leaves and resin, never industrial.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 3 rows and 4 columns: 12 equal square cells, ONE object per cell, centred, filling about 80% of the cell, no shadows on the ground, no text, no labels, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames, no extra objects.
