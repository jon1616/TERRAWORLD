[STYLE SHEET «Roots and Sap» — INVENTORY ICONS] A cohesive set of 12 game inventory icons for a 2D fantasy game whose world is made of living roots, sap and amber. Beautiful HAND-PAINTED digital illustration style (NOT pixel art, NOT 3D render, NOT photo): bold clean readable shapes, soft painterly shading with 3 tones per colour, a gentle highlight, light always coming from the TOP-LEFT, and one thick dark outline (#14101a) around every object. Long objects (weapons, tools, staffs) are drawn DIAGONALLY from bottom-left to top-right, all at the same angle.
Solid flat magenta #FF00FF background everywhere. EXACTLY 3 rows and 4 columns: 12 equal square cells, ONE object per cell, centred, filling about 80% of the cell, no shadows on the ground, NO text, NO labels, NO names under the objects, no numbers, no grid lines, no frames, no extra objects.
